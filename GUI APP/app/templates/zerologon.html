About Zerologon

Zerologon (CVE-2020-1472) is a critical flaw in the Netlogon secure channel that allows unauthenticated attackers to spoof a domain controller and reset the machine account password. Successful exploitation can lead to full domain compromise.

Recommendation

Apply the official Microsoft patch for CVE-2020-1472 and enforce Netlogon secure channel protections. Ensure all domain controllers are updated and monitor for Netlogon secure channel events that indicate spoofing attempts.
